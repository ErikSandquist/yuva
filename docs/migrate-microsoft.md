# Move from Outlook.com or Microsoft 365

Both connect with "Sign in with Microsoft", but the steps differ. Pick the guide by the address of the mailbox:

- An address at `outlook.com`, `hotmail.com` or `live.com` (a personal Microsoft account): follow
  [Move from Outlook.com](migrate-outlook.md).
- A work or school address on your organisation's own domain: follow
  [Move from Microsoft 365](migrate-microsoft365.md).

The overview is in [Move to Yuva](migrate.md).
