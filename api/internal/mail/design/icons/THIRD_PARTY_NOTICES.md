# Social icons

The icons for X, Bluesky, Mastodon, GitHub, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Discord and
Threads are rasterised at 48 × 48 pixels from Simple Icons 15.17.0 (https://simpleicons.org,
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), released under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The brand variants use the colours Simple
Icons lists. The names and logos remain trademarks of their owners; they are shown only to link to a
sender's own profiles.

`linkedin-*.png` and `website-*.png` are Yuva's own drawings, under the license of this directory.
